About L1 and L2 Levels

The application organizes checks into levels that reflect priority and rigor:

  • L1 — foundational security controls that are broadly applicable and low impact.
  • L2 — stricter controls for hardened environments; may affect usability or require change management.

You can filter or bulk-select by level using the controls on the left. Results display level per item and contribute to the overall score.

Tip: Hover table rows to review details, and use the comparison feature to see status changes between runs.
